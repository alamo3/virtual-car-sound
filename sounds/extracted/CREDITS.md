# Credits: sounds extracted from Assetto Corsa car mods

These engine, shift and backfire sounds were extracted (with `fsb_extract.py`) from the
FMOD sound banks of the Assetto Corsa car mods below, and are used by the "Loop Engine"
voices in `loopengine.py`. All rights remain with the mod authors (and with the original
creators of any sounds the mods include). Used here with credit, for a personal,
non-commercial project; contact us if you are an author and want them credited
differently or removed.

| Folder | Mod | Author | Version |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gue_bmw_m3_e46_gtr/` | BMW M3 E46 GTR | GUERILLA Mods | 0.9 |
| `fx_audi_r8_gt_evo/` | Audi R8 GT EVO | FX — https://www.patreon.com/fxmods | 1.2 |
| `bsk_hyundai_ioniq5_n/` | Hyundai IONIQ 5 N | B_SK | 1.0 |

Only the files the voices actually load are kept. `loops.json` in each folder holds the
loop points read from the bank.
